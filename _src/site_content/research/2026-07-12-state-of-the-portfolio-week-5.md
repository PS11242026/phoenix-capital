## Leading, but read it correctly

Five and a half weeks in, the portfolio is **+7.3%** against the S&P 500 at **−0.3%** — a +7.6pp active gap. Before anyone annualizes that: the daily win-rate vs the index is only ~50%. The lead is a few names re-rating hard — QNST (+42% since entry), FLO, YELP, CAG — not broad daily dominance. That is exactly the shape this signal's edge takes: long stretches of nothing, then discrete re-ratings when the market closes a gap.
