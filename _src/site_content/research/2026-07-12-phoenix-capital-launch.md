Phoenix Capital publishes one concentrated model portfolio built on one idea: **markets misprice boring, forecastable cash flows more persistently than they misprice stories.**

## The signal

Every name in a validated universe gets a full, conservative DCF from point-in-time fundamentals. The gap between intrinsic value and market price — the *discount* — is ranked within sector families and weighted by how reliably that family's discounts have predicted forward returns. The ten widest, most reliable gaps form the portfolio, equal-weight.

## The evidence, stated the way we'd want it stated to us

- Backtest, 42 quarters, net of costs: **+4.3%/yr alpha vs the S&P 500** (beta 1.43), +5.6%/yr vs the Nasdaq 100.
- The caveat we repeat on every page: the backtest universe is survivorship-biased. The robust statistics are the long-short spread (**+15.6%/yr**) and top-10 minus universe (**+7.4%/yr**).
- The live record is weeks old. We publish it anyway, daily, because a track record you can watch accrue is worth more than a backtest you have to trust.

## The rules

1. Positions change only at scheduled rebalances, with the rationale published the same day.
2. Posts are never edited after publication; corrections get their own dated post.
3. Every figure carries its basis: backtest vs live, which benchmark, which beta.
4. If the live record decays past its historical drawdown envelope, the portfolio gets cut — and the post explaining why stays up.

> This is a paper book, not a fund. Keeping it that way is what keeps this work independent — and what lets us publish the ugly weeks with the good ones.
