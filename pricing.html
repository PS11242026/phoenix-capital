About this project

A portfolio project, not a business

Phoenix Capital is a demonstration site for my DCF Valuation Platform: a Kalman-filtered, state-space DCF engine whose intrinsic values drive a concentrated model (paper) portfolio. Nothing here is for sale, no subscriptions exist, and no client money is managed.
